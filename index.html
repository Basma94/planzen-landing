Before you start

The questions people ask.

What is Planzen?

A planning app for your money. You enter what comes in and what's owed, and Planzen tells you what's genuinely left to spend — before the month happens, not after.

How does Planzen work?

Bills and income are rules, not one-off entries. Add rent once, mark it as repeating, and every future month already accounts for it. Available updates as you go.

Is Planzen a budgeting app?

It does budgeting, but the point is planning ahead rather than categorising what already happened. Most budgeting apps look backward; Planzen is built to look forward.

How is Planzen different from an expense tracker?

A tracker records a purchase after it happens. Planzen plans the month before it starts, then tracks what actually happened against that plan.

How does Planzen calculate available spending?

Income for the month, minus every expense still due — paid or not. That figure is "Available." A separate figure, "Balance," shows what's left after only what you've actually paid.

Is Planzen free?

Yes — the Planner, Assets, Goals and Activity screens are free with no limit. A paid tier adds the AI advisor and logging by typing a sentence.
